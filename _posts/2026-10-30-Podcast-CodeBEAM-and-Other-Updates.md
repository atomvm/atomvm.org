Last week was an exciting one for AtomVM! A new podcast episode about running Gleam on microcontrollers and a talk at Code BEAM Europe!

<!--more-->

## Gleam on microcontrollers

On Friday, October 23, [Davide Bettio](https://uninstall.it/) and [Giacomo Cavalieri](https://www.giacomocavalieri.me/) joined **BEAM There, Done That** for a conversation about [running Gleam on microcontrollers with AtomVM](https://beamtheredonethat.com/episodes/embedded-gleam-for-everyone-d-bettio-g-cavalieri-on-microcontrollers).

The episode covers what it takes to run BEAM languages on tiny devices, why Gleam is a great fit for embedded development, and how new tooling makes getting started easier than ever.

Give the [episode a listen](https://beamtheredonethat.com/episodes/embedded-gleam-for-everyone-d-bettio-g-cavalieri-on-microcontrollers)!

## Code BEAM Europe

A couple of days earlier, on Wednesday, October 21, AtomVM was also at **Code BEAM Europe**, with the talk *Updates from the AtomVM Landscape*.

The [slides are now online](https://uninstall.it/slides/Updates-from-the-AtomVM-Landscape.pdf), covering recent developments and what's coming next for AtomVM.

## Bitstrings have landed!

Things are moving fast, and even the Code BEAM Europe slides are already a little outdated: **AtomVM now supports non-byte-aligned bitstrings!**

Thanks to [PR #2357](https://github.com/atomvm/AtomVM/pull/2357), **support for non-byte-aligned bitstrings has been merged into the `release-0.7` branch!**

Another long-awaited feature crossed off the list, and another step toward better BEAM compatibility!
