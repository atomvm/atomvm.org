[Davide Bettio](https://github.com/bettio/) and [Paul Guyot](https://github.com/pguyot/) joined
Allen Wyma and Francesco Cesarini on **BEAM There, Done That** for a conversation about AtomVM and
running BEAM languages on microcontrollers.

<!--more-->

The episode covers what led Davide to build AtomVM, the design trade-offs involved in running
Erlang and Elixir with only a few hundred kilobytes of RAM, concurrency, SMP, and why the BEAM
model can be a good fit for embedded systems.

The conversation also looks at La Machine as a real-world product built on AtomVM, and some of the
lessons learned from running this software on hardware that has to work outside the lab.
